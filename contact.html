What's worth having ready

The date and the hour

Summer weekends fill weeks ahead. Midweek there's almost always room, sometimes the same day.

Count everyone, kids included. That's what decides which boat fits and how many life jackets we load.

Not out of curiosity. A birthday, a bachelorette and a family Sunday are built differently, and they leave at different hours.
