What everybody asks

Miami boat rental FAQs

How much is a boat rental in Miami?

It depends on the boat, the day and how many hours you want. Send us the date on WhatsApp and you get the whole number back — boat, captain and fuel in one figure, before you commit to anything.

How long are the charters?

Four, five or six hours are the usual bookings. Four is enough for the sandbar and the skyline. Six gives you the sandbar, lunch, and the sunset on the way back.

Do I need a captain?

The captain comes with the boat on every LuxSea charter, and fuel is included. You never rent a hull and figure the rest out yourself.

How many people can come?

Up to 13 guests on the Chris Craft 45. The Sundancer 40 and the Amberjack 32 take smaller groups. Tell us your headcount and we'll tell you which boat fits.

Can we bring a cake, food and decorations?

Yes. Birthdays and bachelorettes are most of what we do. Bring the cake, the food, the balloons and your playlist. Tell us in advance and the boat is set up before you step on it.

What's on the boat already?

On the Chris Craft 45: cooler on ice, drinking water, speaker, floating pool mat, a fitted cabin, a bathroom with a shower, fridge and microwave. Captain and fuel included.

Do you speak Spanish?

Yes, and it's how we normally work. The booking, the day and the safety brief run in Spanish or English, whichever your group prefers.
