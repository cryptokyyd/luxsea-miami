Just the two of us

The whole boat, for two people

Sunset on the bay, the cockpit table, a fridge that keeps the wine cold, and a captain who stays at the helm and out of your photographs. It is a private boat, so the only people aboard are the ones you brought.

A boat built for thirteen, carrying two

Most romantic packages in Miami put you on a big boat with other couples and call it private. This is not that. You book the whole boat, and if there are two of you, there are two of you.

That means the bow is yours to lie on, the cockpit table is yours to eat at, and nobody is waiting for you to finish so they can use the space.

Bring the dinner, the table is already there

We do not cater, and we are not going to sell you a set menu we would only be buying from somewhere else. There is a fridge, a cooler on ice, a table in the cockpit and a speaker.

Most couples bring something from a place they already like. If you want the wine cold when you step on, tell us and it goes in the fridge before you arrive.

If you are going to propose, say so when you book

It changes the run. The captain will have the boat facing the right way when the light goes, keep the engine off, and stay out of the frame. If you would rather he took the photograph, he will, and if you would rather he saw nothing at all, that is easier still.

We are not photographers and will not charge you as though we were. If the moment matters that much, bring a friend with a camera and we will find them somewhere to hide.

What everybody asks

Romantic charter questions

Is the boat really private?

Yes. You book the boat, not a seat on it. Nobody else is aboard except the captain, and he stays at the helm.

Can you provide dinner or decorations?

No, and we would rather say so than mark up somebody else's work. Bring the food, the flowers and the playlist. There is a fridge, a cooler on ice and a table in the cockpit, and we will have it all set up before you step on.

What time should we go out for the sunset?

Tell us the date and we will tell you the hour, because it moves through the year. Four hours is the shortest booking, which is comfortably enough to be out there when the light goes and still see the city lit up on the way back.

Can you help with a proposal?

We can point the boat the right way, keep the engine off and stay out of the photographs, and that is genuinely most of it. Say so when you book so the captain plans the run around it.

Tell us the date and what it is for

Anniversary, first date, or the question you have been carrying around for a month. Tell us which, and we will tell you the hour to leave so the light lands where you want it.
